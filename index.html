Daily account-health monitor, US + CA

A daily agent run checks policy issues, notifications and the case log on both marketplaces, cross-checks team chat, captures evidence and drafts a labelled team post, so nothing passes a deadline.

ResultZero policy compliance issues on US and CA with the top health rating. A restricted-products appeal protected about $43K of at-risk sales.

How it works
  1. Opens Account Health for US and CA: policy compliance and health rating.
  2. Reads new performance notifications and seller news.
  3. Opens every case in the log that changed since the last run.
  4. Checks team chat to see whether a teammate already handled each item.
  5. Takes screenshots as evidence.
  6. Drafts “1st half” and “Final review” posts, plus a note on any case nobody owns.
Seller CentralGoogle ChatBrowser automationScheduled tasks

Escalated-cases tracker

One sheet and a scheduled agent track every case escalated to the marketplace’s account manager, so stalled cases surface before they go cold.

ResultOn day one it caught a case marked “Needs Escalation” that had already been escalated. Resolved cases now move to their own tab automatically.

How it works
  1. Consolidated account-manager emails, the case log and monthly primary-case threads.
  2. Rebuilt the sheet with a status dropdown, formula flags, row shading and a Resolved tab.
  3. A scheduled agent reads Seller Central, Gmail and every team-chat space, then updates the sheet.
  4. Each run reports changes, what needs action from us, and what has been open too long, with a suggested next push.
  5. Flags: red at 30+ days escalated, yellow when waiting on us, bold after 7+ days without a marketplace update.
Google SheetsGmailSeller CentralGoogle Chat

Sourcing-cost proof of value

A weekly workflow recovers under-valued reimbursements by submitting the true invoice cost with a highlighted proof PDF, instead of accepting the portal’s lower estimate.

Result8 items approved and 1 pending in a single weekly run, from 424 screened. Earlier denials traced to a missing ID on the proof, then fixed.

How it works
  1. Pulls the portal status list and finds items not yet approved.
  2. Splits bundles and multi-packs into their component SKUs.
  3. Finds the highest-cost received PO in the inventory system and the matching invoice.
  4. Highlights the invoice line and stamps an ASIN–SKU label with Python (PyMuPDF).
  5. Calculates cost: unit, sum of parts, or unit × quantity.
  6. Submits the cost with the PDF and logs the result. Originals are never edited; denials retry after 30 days.
Seller CentralSellercloudmonday.comPython · PyMuPDF

Unattended vendor-portal pipeline

Every evening it exports four reports from a retailer’s vendor portal, validates them, emails them to the client, then audits delivery with an automatic backup send.

Result4 of 4 files delivered daily, the largest about 35,700 rows. It alerts only when something fails and never sends duplicates.

How it works
  1. 7 PM: archives yesterday’s files, logs in with a saved session and exports three report cards to CSV.
  2. Queues the large inventory export, polls until it succeeds, then downloads and unzips it.
  3. Validates every file for size, expected header and freshness, and writes a READY.json manifest.
  4. 8 PM: a local script emails each file to the client’s data inbox.
  5. 9 PM: checks Gmail Sent for every attachment, re-downloads anything missing and sends it by a backup route.

Walmart and beauty-marketplace checks

I took over the Walmart 3P and beauty-retail marketplace channels: start-of-day, midday and end-of-day checks on price, Buy Box, stock and content, backed by a living handover document.

Result22 of 22 prices matched, Buy Box held on 17 of 17 in-stock items, 3 content issues flagged and a third-party hijacker escalated to brand protection.

How it works
  1. Daily posts with a check table for prices, Buy Box, stock, image counts, 1P items and catalog errors.
  2. Weekly: seller-tier metrics against thresholds, published and unpublished catalog counts, removal orders.
  3. Live pages audited for target price, Buy Box owner, suppression and placeholder images.
  4. Live titles compared with the title-tracking sheet.
  5. Handover document rebuilt from two months of team chat: schedule, templates, who decides what, open items.

Deal-event listing auditor

A reusable tool audits every deal listing before and during Prime Day-style events, where ordinary scraping is blocked.

ResultFive checks per listing (deal badge, price within ±$0.05, strikethrough, Buy Box owner, suppression), with PASS, WARN, ISSUE or ERROR verdicts in a colour-coded Excel summary.

How it works
  1. A Python builder turns the deals sheet into a browser userscript, cleaning invalid and duplicate rows.
  2. The userscript walks each listing in a logged-in browser with throttling, captcha pause and resume, and retries.
  3. Results export to CSV; a Python formatter builds a colour-coded Excel file with a Summary sheet.
PythonJavaScript userscriptExcel

Appeals, cases and data disputes

Evidence-led cases and strategy-first appeals that get past support scripts, from pricing-error disputes to brand authorization letters.

ResultProved a brand-analytics report over-counted units about 5× (≈12.4K real against 48.7K reported). The marketplace confirmed the defect, fixed it and committed to a historical correction.

How it works
  1. Appeal framework: admit with a full plan of action, dispute with evidence, or reframe the characterization.
  2. 300–450 words: issue, root cause, corrective steps, preventive steps, evidence and a one-sentence ask.
  3. Pricing-error disputes backed by regular pricing at 3–4 retailers and the marketplace’s own policy.
  4. A Letter of Authorization answering each rejection reason clause by clause.
  5. Cosmetic-kit labeling research (21 CFR 701.3, MoCRA) for a new bundle launch.
Seller CentralBusiness ReportsExcel

SOPs, PPC builder and quality gate

Know-how turned into repeatable process: shipment SOPs, a PPC keyword workbook generator and a quality gate on every outbound message.

ResultAn 82-frame training recording became a six-step FBA inbound SOP. A keyword export becomes a four-sheet campaign workbook in one step.

How it works
  1. FBA inbound SOP: replenishment file, classify rows (regular or hazmat), up to four shipments, case quantities (max 150 units), prep and expiry, log on the work board.
  2. PPC builder: up to six semantic keyword groups, 20 single-keyword campaign targets, negatives in five categories and a strategy summary.
  3. Quality gate: cases, appeals and team posts are scored, fixed and re-scored before they go out.
  4. A 10-day learning plan to hand over a Buy Box suppression check: shadow, review, then solo.
